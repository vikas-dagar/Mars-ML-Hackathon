Problem

Safety is the cost.

100 people. Two years. Habitat, water, depot, landing zone — vehicles still have to move.

Earth routing scores kilometres. On Mars the unsafe kilometre is the one that does not arrive.

100 people · 24 months · 0 roads

Safety has to price

  • Terrain

    Sand and rock spike rolling resistance and slip. A crater is not a shortcut.

  • Solar

    Morning elevation ≠ afternoon. Canyon walls and crater floors get mostly diffuse skylight.

  • Energy

    Cargo on a slope is not a linear extra. A sunlit hop can make energy while you drive.

  • Dust

    Optical depth eats insolation and traction. Hazard is a scored layer.

Solution

Google Maps for Mars.

MapForMars. A route optimizer. Same two points, a mission, and a load — the safe geometry, not the short one.
